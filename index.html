Who We Are

A platform for collective progress

RIC is envisioned as a non-profit, non-political community platform dedicated to unity, education, social welfare, professional development, economic empowerment and humanitarian service.

🎯

Our Vision

To build a connected, educated, skilled, dignified and globally engaged Rayeen community that contributes positively to society and humanity.

🧭

Our Mission

To create practical networks, programmes and digital systems that help community members access education, opportunities, mentorship, welfare support and meaningful connections.

Our Focus
